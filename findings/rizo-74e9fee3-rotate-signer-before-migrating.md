## Summary

`Rizo` at `0x74e9fee3fcb56bccac22e726cce7a78ca90185e1`. It accepts signatures from a key that a function can replace; the holder rotates it before the account takes code.

## Mechanism

PQ-17. Once that key's account has real code its signatures stop counting; its holder rotates first, or the owner afterwards.
