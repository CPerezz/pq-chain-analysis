## Summary

`MongsToken` at `0xb4a7d131436ed8ec06ad696fa3bf8d23c0ab3acf`. It accepts signatures from a key that a function can replace; the holder rotates it before the account takes code.

## Mechanism

PQ-17. Once that key's account has real code its signatures stop counting; its holder rotates first, or the owner afterwards.
