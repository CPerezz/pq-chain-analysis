## Summary

`AuctionityDepositEth` at `0xd01c92937400dd1ece24992b1dc44aeaa47ae72a` (Etherscan tag: Auctionity: Old Address). It accepts signatures from a key that a function can replace; the holder rotates it before the account takes code.

## Mechanism

PQ-17. Once that key's account has real code its signatures stop counting; its holder rotates first, or the owner afterwards.
