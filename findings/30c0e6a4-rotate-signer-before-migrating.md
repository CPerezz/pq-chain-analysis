## Summary

Contract `0x30c0e6a4b247e706d3a75b6e88bb0645c3b39757`. It accepts signatures from a key that a function can replace; the holder rotates it before the account takes code.

## Mechanism

PQ-17. Once that key's account has real code its signatures stop counting; its holder rotates first, or the owner afterwards.
