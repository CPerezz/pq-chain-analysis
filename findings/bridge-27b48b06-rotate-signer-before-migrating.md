## Summary

`Bridge` at `0x27b48b0633d0cecf9701c1a99f406fed5514ac88`. It accepts signatures from a key that a function can replace; the holder rotates it before the account takes code.

## Mechanism

PQ-17. Once that key's account has real code its signatures stop counting; its holder rotates first, or the owner afterwards.
